\chapterstub{%
\textit{[Opis do przetłumaczenia --- patrz wydanie angielskie.]}

Argument: every AI SDK in Python is the same three things \dash{} a pydantic
model, an httpx client and a streaming iterator \dash{} and knowing that
makes the SDKs readable rather than magical. pydantic for structured
outputs: a JSON schema from a model, \code{model\_validate\_json}, strict
against lax; httpx clients for model APIs: timeouts, retries, streaming
responses, and the \code{AsyncClient} lifetime from chapter 8; the shape of
the Anthropic and OpenAI SDKs \dash{} client, then messages or responses,
then typed results, then streaming \dash{} read from the installed, pinned
package and never from memory; notebooks against scripts, when a notebook is
right and why nothing in this book is one; tokens and cost as first-class
quantities. Pointer, never duplication: agents, tools, middleware and graphs
belong to the LangChain book, and this chapter stops at one model call and
one structured output. Payoff: a \code{call\_model} function with typed
input and typed output the reader can drop into the chapter 9 service.
Measurement: experiment E8, validation cost of one structured output across
pydantic strict, pydantic lax and a dataclass over \code{json}, free. Four
exercises.
