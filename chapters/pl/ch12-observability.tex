\chapter{Obserwowalność i operacje: structlog, OpenTelemetry, kontenery i audyty}
\label{ch:observability}

\chapterstub{%
\textit{[Opis do przetłumaczenia --- patrz wydanie angielskie.]}

Argument: the .NET engineer's operational reflexes are right, and the Python
tools that satisfy them exist; the gap is their names and their defaults.
The standard \code{logging} module against \code{ILogger}, its configuration
model and why people replace it; structlog against structured \code{ILogger}
scopes; correlation identifiers through \code{contextvars} against
\code{AsyncLocal}; OpenTelemetry in Python against .NET \dash{} auto-
instrumentation, exporters, resource attributes; container images built with
uv (multi-stage, \code{uv sync --frozen --no-dev}, a non-root user, the two
environment variables everybody sets); \code{pip-audit} against \code{dotnet
list package --vulnerable}; health and readiness checks; graceful shutdown
through signals and the lifespan; secrets through \code{pydantic-settings}
again. Payoff: a Dockerfile and a logging-and-tracing bootstrap the reader
can copy. Measurement: experiment E7, image size and cold start of three
Dockerfile shapes, free. Four exercises.
